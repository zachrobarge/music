03 / About

Music has always been
about possibilities.

Zach Robarge is a woodwind doubler and educator based in Denver, Colorado. He specializes in saxophones, flute/piccolo, clarinets, and oboe, and has performed across classical, jazz, popular music, theater, and chamber settings.

Zach holds degrees in saxophone performance and music education, along with a performance certificate in flute and oboe studies, from the University of Massachusetts. He previously served as an Artist in Residence at UMass, teaching saxophone while his mentor was on sabbatical.

Over more than a decade of private teaching, Zach has worked with hundreds of students from elementary school through adulthood. His students have earned solo and leadership roles, participated in regional and honor ensembles, and gone on to collegiate music programs.
